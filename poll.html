🙏 About Us

Home of Christ Prayer Ministry is a Christ-centered community committed to helping people grow in their relationship with God, serve others, and share the message of Jesus Christ.

📖 Our Mission

Our mission is to:

  • Share the Gospel of Jesus Christ
  • Build a strong community of believers
  • Encourage spiritual growth
  • Serve our community
  • Help people discover and fulfill their God-given purpose
